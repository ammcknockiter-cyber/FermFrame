=====================================================================
  FERMFRAME INSTALLER
  Turns a Geekmagic "Smart Weather Clock" into a little screen that
  shows your beer's fermentation graph from Brewfather or a RAPT Pill.
  Once it's set up, your PC doesn't need to stay on.
=====================================================================

WHAT YOU NEED
  - A Geekmagic "Smart Weather Clock" (the small cube with a colour
    screen; not the "SmallTV Pro").
  - A Windows 10 or 11 PC or laptop WITH WI-FI.
  - Your home Wi-Fi name and password. Most clocks need 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi.
  - Brewfather: your User ID and an API key
      (Brewfather > Settings > API > Generate API key, tick "Read batches")
    or RAPT: your RAPT email and an API secret
      (RAPT website > My account > API Secrets > Create)

HOW TO DO IT (about 5 minutes)
  1. Unzip this folder (right-click > Extract All).
  2. Plug the clock into power. A USB phone charger is fine.
     It does NOT need a cable to the PC.
  3. Double-click  "FermFrame Installer.bat".
     If Windows asks whether to run it, choose "More info" > "Run anyway"
     (or "Run").
  4. Fill in your Wi-Fi and your Brewfather or RAPT details.
  5. Press "Set up my clock" and wait. Your PC leaves its Wi-Fi for a
     minute or two while it talks to the clock. That's normal, and it
     comes back by itself.
  6. Done! The graph appears on the clock.

AFTERWARDS
  - Change settings: open  http://fermframe.local  in a browser, or
    scan the QR code that shows on the clock when it needs fixing.
  - A new brew: set the batch to "Fermenting" in Brewfather and the
    clock picks it up within 5 minutes.
  - Brewfather keeps ONE API key per account. If you ever press
    "Generate API key" again, the old key stops working: put the new
    key on every clock (open http://fermframe.local or scan the QR code).
    Several clocks can share the same key.
  - Power cut? The clock reconnects by itself. If the router is slow
    to come back, the clock keeps retrying every few minutes.

MAKE IT YOURS
  Open http://fermframe.local on your phone or PC and scroll to
  "Look & feel": themes, your own colours, font, brightness, dimming at
  night, graph style (line, filled, dots, daily bars), time range,
  rotating pages (graph, big number, clock, your other brews) and a
  "Ready to keg?" badge. The picture at the top shows what the clock
  will look like before you press Save.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
  "Can't find the clock"
     Unplug the clock, count to 5, plug it in, wait 30 seconds and
     press the button again. Keep the clock near the PC.
  "Windows needs Location turned on"
     Windows 11 only shows Wi-Fi networks to programs when Location is
     on. Turn on "Location services" and "Let desktop apps access your
     location", then try again.
  The clock says "Can't find Wi-Fi"
     The Wi-Fi name or password was wrong, or your Wi-Fi is 5 GHz only.
     Scan the QR code on the clock with your phone and fix it there.
  The clock says "wrong ID or API key" / "wrong email or API secret"
     Make a new key or secret and run the installer again.
  The clock says "No Fermenting batch in Brewfather"
     Set your batch's status to Fermenting in Brewfather.

GOOD TO KNOW
  - This replaces the clock's weather-clock software. Your details are
    stored only on the clock.
  - FermFrame is homemade and isn't made by Geekmagic, Brewfather,
    RAPT or KegLand. Use it at your own risk.
