## FermFrame 1.0.2

**Have 1.0.1?** The app offers this update by itself – click **Update now** in the bar at the top.
**New, or on 1.0.0?** Download `FermFrame-1.0.2.exe` below, open it and click **Install** (or **Update**).
Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't signed – click **More info → Run anyway**.

**You need:** a Brewfather **Premium** account (30-day free trial for new accounts), or a RAPT account with
**RAPT Premium** (about $15 a year; RAPT's docs list API access as a Premium feature).

### Changed
- The app now says clearly, where you'll see it, that Brewfather and RAPT need a paid plan for the connection
  FermFrame uses: a one-time **Good to know** card when the app opens, and a short note next to the
  Brewfather/RAPT choice in **Set up a new FermFrame** and on the **Settings** tab.
