# FermFrame video script

One audio file per scene, named `01.mp3` … `14.mp3` (or `.wav`), in `docs\video\voice\`.
The video is timed to each recording, so the exact length doesn't matter.
Everything on screen comes from the app's demo mode (made-up FermFrames).

| # | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Title card: **FermFrame** – *Your fermentation, at a glance* | This is FermFrame. It turns a cheap desktop clock into a little screen that shows your fermentation, straight from Brewfather or a RAPT Pill. Let's take a look at the app. |
| 02 | The install window; the pointer moves to **Install** | Download FermFrame from its GitHub page and open it. Click Install. It installs just for you, with no admin rights, and adds itself to the Start menu. |
| 03 | Main window; the pointer moves down the list | This is the main window. On the left are your FermFrames. A green dot means it's working, and grey means it isn't answering. |
| 04 | The pointer moves across the four tiles | For the one you've selected, you see the brew, its gravity, the temperature, the alcohol so far, and how many days it's been fermenting. |
| 05 | The pointer glides along the graph | Underneath is the whole fermentation as a graph. Gravity in amber, temperature in blue. |
| 06 | Click **Brew closet**; point at **Ready to keg?** | Click another FermFrame to see its brew. When the gravity hasn't moved for three days, it lets you know it might be ready to keg. |
| 07 | **Rename** → type *Stout corner* → OK | Give each one a name that makes sense to you. |
| 08 | **Settings** tab → theme **Dark** → drag **Brightness** | The Settings tab changes everything about the screen. Pick a theme or your own colours, a font, the brightness, and when to dim at night. |
| 09 | Scroll to **Graph** and **Pages**; tick **Big number** | Choose the graph style and time range, and which pages it takes turns showing. The graph, a big number you can read across the room, the time, or your other brews. |
| 10 | **Save to FermFrame** → *Saved* | Nothing changes until you press Save. Then the screen updates straight away. |
| 11 | **Firmware** tab → **Reinstall** → progress → done | The Firmware tab keeps everything up to date. One click, about a minute, and it keeps all its settings. |
| 12 | **Buy another FermFrame** window; point at the shop buttons | Want another one? This shows exactly which clock to buy, and which ones won't work. |
| 13 | **Set up a new FermFrame**: type ID and key → **Set up my FermFrame** → messages scroll | When it arrives, plug it into power and click Set up a new FermFrame. Check your Wi-Fi, add your Brewfather details, and press the button. A few minutes later, your brew is on the screen. |
| 14 | Title card: **FermFrame** – *Free and open source · github.com/ammcknockiter-cyber/FermFrame* | That's FermFrame. It's free and open source, and the full manual is on GitHub. Happy brewing. |
