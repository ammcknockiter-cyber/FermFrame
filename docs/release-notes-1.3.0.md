## FermFrame 1.3.0 – now on Android too

**Windows, have 1.0.1 or later?** The app offers this update by itself – click **Update now** in the bar at the top.
**Windows, new or on 1.0.0?** Download `FermFrame-1.3.0.exe` below, open it and click **Install** (or **Update**).
Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't signed – click **More info → Run anyway**.

**Android:** on your phone, tap `FermFrame-1.3.0.apk` below, open it and tap **Install**. Android asks to allow installing
apps from your browser the first time; Play Protect may say it doesn't know the app – **More details → Install anyway**.

Then update your FermFrames on the **Firmware** tab (FermFrame 2.5.0 is built into both apps).

### New
- **The FermFrame app for Android.** Everything the Windows app does – see your FermFrames, the graph, change every
  setting with a live preview, update firmware, set up new clocks – from your phone. Use either app, or both.
- **Alerts** on your phone: ready to keg, temperature outside a range you choose, a problem on the FermFrame, or one that
  stops answering.
- **Home-screen widget** with your brews' gravity and temperature.
- **Share this brew** – a picture of your gravity, temperature, ABV and graph to send to friends.
- **Names live on the FermFrame** (firmware 2.5): rename it once and every PC and phone shows the same name.
- **Setup mode waits 15 minutes** when you open it on purpose (plugging in 3 times), instead of 3.
- Setting up from the phone fills in your Wi-Fi name, and a FermFrame that's already set up keeps its saved Wi-Fi password
  and account details if you leave them blank.
