## FermFrame 1.1.0 – preview and security

**Have 1.0.1 or later?** The app offers this update by itself – click **Update now** in the bar at the top.
**New, or on 1.0.0?** Download `FermFrame-1.1.0.exe` below, open it and click **Install** (or **Update**).
Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't signed – click **More info → Run anyway**.

Then update your FermFrames on the **Firmware** tab (FermFrame 2.3.0 is built in).

**You need:** a Brewfather **Premium** account (30-day free trial for new accounts), or a RAPT account with
**RAPT Premium** (about $15 a year; RAPT's docs list API access as a Premium feature).

### New
- **See it before you save.** The Settings tab has a live **Preview** of the FermFrame's screen beside the settings. Change
  the theme, colours, font, brightness or graph and it redraws straight away, with your brew's latest readings. Buttons
  switch between the Graph, Big number, Clock and Other brew pages.
- **Settings password (optional).** Stops anyone else on your Wi-Fi changing a FermFrame's settings or installing other
  firmware on it. Set it on the Settings tab (section 7) or when setting up a new FermFrame. The app remembers it,
  encrypted for your Windows account. Forgot it? Plug the FermFrame in 3 times in a row to open **Setup mode**.
- **The setup Wi-Fi has a password.** *FermFrame-Setup* is no longer open: each FermFrame makes up its own password and
  shows it on its screen (and inside its QR code, so phones still join with one scan). The app asks for it once during setup.
- **[Is FermFrame safe?](https://github.com/ammcknockiter-cyber/FermFrame/blob/main/docs/SECURITY.md)** – a plain-words
  page on what FermFrame can and can't get at.
