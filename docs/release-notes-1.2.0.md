## FermFrame 1.2.0 – your own hydrometer, free

**Have 1.0.1 or later?** The app offers this update by itself – click **Update now** in the bar at the top.
**New, or on 1.0.0?** Download `FermFrame-1.2.0.exe` below, open it and click **Install** (or **Update**).
Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't signed – click **More info → Run anyway**.

Then update your FermFrames on the **Firmware** tab (FermFrame 2.4.0 is built in).

### New
- **My own hydrometer – no account, no subscription.** An **iSpindel** or **GravityMon** can now send its readings
  straight to a FermFrame over your Wi-Fi. The FermFrame keeps about six weeks of readings and draws the graph,
  OG, ABV, days and "Ready to keg?" from them. Choose **My own hydrometer** when setting up or on the Settings tab;
  the app shows exactly what to type into the hydrometer (Server, Port 80, Path `/ispindel`).
- SG or Plato, °C or °F – all converted. Readings out of the liquid are ignored.
- **Start a new brew** clears the graph for the next batch; the FermFrame also notices by itself when the hydrometer
  goes into new wort.

Brewfather and RAPT keep working as before (they need Brewfather Premium / RAPT Premium).
